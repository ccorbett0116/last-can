# Feedback form (1% bonus)

The form itself is on **Brightspace**. This page only shows what it asks, so
you know what to expect.

It is **anonymous**: we see the answers, not who gave them. Brightspace only
tells us how many people submitted.

## The three questions

1. **What did you like about this lab?**
2. **What didn't you like about this lab?**
3. **Any other comments?**

All three are free text, and all three are optional.

## The bonus

- If at least **60% of the class** submits the form, **everyone in the class
  gets a 1% bonus**.
- A blank form still counts toward the 60%.
- There is no penalty if the class falls short.

Honest answers are the useful ones. This lab is run again next year, and what
you write decides what changes.
